Return & Refund Policy

Last Modified: June 24, 2026

Policy Sections

  • 1. Return Window
  • 2. Refund Processing
  • 3. Shipping Labels

1. 30-Day Return Window

We offer a hassle-free 30-day return policy for all qualifying electronics and gadgets. Items must be returned in their original packaging, unused, and with all warranty cards intact to verify security tracking parameters.

2. Refund Processing Currency

All items are refunded in US Dollars (USD) or Canadian Dollars (CAD) directly back to your original payment method. Once parsed at our regional hub facilities, credit distribution timelines take 3-5 business days via Stripe or PayPal transaction nodes.

3. Return Shipping Labels

Customers across the United States and Canada can generate a pre-paid return label through our automated online Help Center dashboard. Returned items are dispatched securely via domestic carriers like UPS, FedEx, or Canada Post directly back to our domestic fulfillment hubs.
